## Why

llame injects server-authored context into the model's view through four mechanisms that share no contract: `data-model-context` rendered as `<system-reminder>`, `data-tool-availability` as `<runtime-tool-availability>`, `data-recency-digest` as `<chat-recency-update>`, and the compaction checkpoint as `<conversation-checkpoint>`. Three tag vocabularies, three `kind` fields meaning different things, one `version` field on one of the three, and no owner for the rail itself — placement, ordering, provenance framing, escaping, truncation disclosure, and compaction treatment are each restated per producer or absent entirely. The model is never told what any of this is: the packaged prompt explains the personalization and chat-history blocks and says nothing about the envelope prepended to the user's own message.

The costs are already being paid. `UUID_PATTERN` and `isExactRecord` exist in four copies across two apps, two of them sorting differently. `apps/web/app/(chat)/components/chat-page.tsx` carries a hand-maintained swallow-list that must name every server-authored part type or print `unsupported part type: …` into the owner's transcript. And because the persisted parts are validated by exact key set, `apps/api/AGENTS.md` makes every new producer a coordinated API/worker revision boundary. Skills, agentic instruction files, #408's temporal block, and #198's episodic recall are all queued behind that tax, and VISION.md already commits to the shape they need: _"Any surface that injects, withdraws, summarizes, or reorders context must be able to say so, and must be disclosable to the owner… each is the same shape and inherits the same rule."_

## What Changes

- **One capability owns the rail.** `context-injection` owns the envelope, ordering, provenance framing, escaping, compaction re-baseline, and the receipt record. Producers keep owning when their item fires and what it says.
- **Two independent axes, plus residency.** `producer` answers who authored an item; `form` answers what kind of thing it is; `residency` (`prefix`, `rail`) answers where the fact lives. The form vocabulary ships only the values that have a producer — `notice`, `snapshot`, `checkpoint` — with anticipated forms recorded as research provenance rather than specified ahead of an implementation. Form is semantic: presentation is derived from it and never encoded into it, and an unrecognized form or producer is tolerated rather than rejected.
- **Residency is the normative extension contract.** A complete statement of current state that changes less often than compaction is prefix-resident; an account of something that happened is rail-resident; state that changes more often than compaction is a frozen prefix baseline plus rail deltas re-baked at compaction — the digest's shipped idiom, named as the general pattern rather than left as digest-specific cleverness. A prefix change is silent to the model by default and earns a rail notice only when history was conditioned on the old value: **announce assertional changes, stay silent on behavioral ones.**
- **BREAKING: one persisted part type.** `data-model-context`, `data-tool-availability`, and `data-recency-digest` collapse into `data-context` carrying `{ v, producer, form, runId, payload }`. No reshape and no compatibility layer: a cleanup migration removes the legacy part shapes from existing `messages.parts` rows outright. No llame instance holds data worth preserving through this boundary, so paying for a reshape would buy nothing.
- **Strict envelope, strict known payloads, opaque unknown producer.** Exact-key-set validation is kept for the envelope and for known payloads — it is what makes client-authored metadata unforgeable — but an unknown `producer` SHALL parse, SHALL be recorded in the receipt, and SHALL render as nothing, and an unrecognized `form` is treated as absent. This converts a future producer from a coordinated revision boundary into an additive change, and turns a version-skew drop from silent into declared and fail-closed.
- **A fixed producer precedence order** for co-occurring items, stated once in the rail's specification and extended there when a producer is added, replacing the ordering sentence currently carried by `tool-calling`.
- **One `<system-reminder>` envelope, one text block per item.** Every item renders as `<system-reminder producer="…" form="…">` with a single unremovable line stating it was not authored by the user. Each item is its own `text` content block inside the triggering user message rather than concatenated into one string, so the boundary between server-authored framing and user text is structural instead of a `\n\n` convention the user can imitate. `<runtime-tool-availability>` and `<chat-recency-update>` cease to exist as tag names.
- **The compaction checkpoint adopts the rail's rendering without moving its storage.** It keeps the `compactions` table — whose `parentId` lineage and `uptoSeq` supersession query cannot be expressed as a message part — and its standalone leading-message placement, but renders as `producer: 'compaction'`, `form: 'checkpoint'` under the shared envelope with the shared framing, escaping, and precedence rules.
- **Model switch becomes one cause of `effective-context-change`.** The producer is renamed to the general concept its `kind: 'model_switch'` discriminator always anticipated, with a closed cause vocabulary of which `model` is the single member that has a shipped detector. One cause per item; the notice never enumerates dimensions; a dimension owned by another producer is reported by that producer, never duplicated.
- **Every item declares its own precedence,** as the packaged prompt already does twice for the personalization and chat-history blocks. With owner-authored skills and repo-authored instruction files arriving, an un-ranked injected instruction is a privilege-escalation surface rather than a style question.
- **Reserved tag names are escaped on the user and tool rails at render.** `sanitizeAuthoredText` already implements the discipline and already reserves `system-reminder`. It is already applied to owner-authored config, digest titles, admitted MCP tool declarations, and replayed tool observations. Two gaps remain: **visible user text**, which reaches the model unescaped, and a **tool result returned during the turn that produced it**, which reaches the model before the replay path that would neutralize it. Assistant output is deliberately excluded: a model does not treat its own prior turns as authoritative, and llame's own users legitimately discuss llame's prompt architecture, so escaping replayed assistant text would corrupt real content to defend a non-authoritative path.
- **The packaged prompt gains a section describing the envelope.** Framing lives in two places because an operator replaces the prompt wholesale via `systemPromptFile`: the envelope self-identifies structurally, and the prompt explains the convention additively.
- **A per-run record of rendered items.** `runs.context_items` stores what was actually rendered, inheriting `runs_owner` RLS and owner-only visibility. Rendered items cannot live in `model_context_snapshots`, which is content-addressed and reused while rail items vary per turn with all four hashed inputs identical. Storage lands here; no endpoint and no UI.
- **Compaction re-baseline is stated once and inherited,** replacing the digest's re-bake rule and tool availability's epoch rule being derived independently.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `context-injection`: the rail itself — the `<system-reminder>` envelope and its unremovable provenance framing, the `producer`/`form`/`residency` vocabulary and its unknown-value tolerance, one text block per item inside the triggering user message, total ordering across co-occurring items, the two lifecycles (a durable part exists or it does not), the residency decision procedure that governs prefix re-render versus rail append, per-item precedence declaration, reserved-tag escaping on the user and tool rails, the compaction re-baseline rule, the per-run rendered-item record, and the disclosed non-erasure property of items carrying content sourced from outside the chat.

### Modified Capabilities

- `model-system-prompts`: the compaction summarization instruction's digest exclusion is rewritten to name the shared envelope plus the excluded producer, because a unified envelope leaves no per-producer delimiter to name, and the checkpoint wrapper stops naming the retired `conversation-checkpoint` delimiter — without both, the non-erasure defect that exclusion exists to prevent is reintroduced; the literal quoted model-switch reminder block is replaced by a reference to the rail's envelope; the persisted `data-model-context` part becomes `data-context` with `producer: 'effective-context-change'` and a closed single-valued `cause`; and the packaged prompt gains the envelope-describing section. The effective-context receipt is **not** changed by this change: the per-Run record of injected items is written but has no read surface yet, per design.md's Non-Goals.
- `tool-calling`: availability disclosure keeps its transition semantics, its closed reason codes, and its epoch behavior, but cedes rail mechanics — envelope name, exact headings, placement, and the switch-then-availability ordering sentence — to the rail spec, and its persisted part becomes `data-context` with `producer: 'tool-availability'`.
- `chat-recency-digest`: the appended-events requirement cedes the envelope and slot to the rail spec, the supersession marker is expressed through the rail's `snapshot` form instead of a bespoke marker shape, and the digest's baseline-plus-deltas lifecycle is restated as an instance of the rail's residency rule rather than as its own invention.

## Impact

- **Code**: `apps/api/src/chats/{model-context-part,tool-availability-part,recency-digest-part}.ts` collapse into one validated part module with a single kernel; `apps/api/src/chats/context-builder.ts` (envelope rendering, block-per-item assembly, ordering, replay escaping); `apps/api/src/chats/chat-loop.service.ts` (rendered-item recording at bind time); `apps/api/src/prompts/chat-default.md`; `apps/api/src/instance-config/authored-text.ts` (reserved set loses exactly the three retired rail delimiters, keeping every unrelated entry; new call sites on the user and tool rails).
- **Web**: `apps/web/lib/services/chat/history.ts` (its own `UUID_PATTERN`, `isExactRecord`, and `isModelSwitchPart` are keyed to the old shape) and `apps/web/app/(chat)/components/chat-page.tsx` (per-part-type branches). Only the minimum needed to keep existing surfaces working — no new context is surfaced in the UI by this change (#464, #465).
- **Database**: one hand-authored cleanup migration stripping legacy context parts from `messages.parts`, recorded in the `apps/api/AGENTS.md` migration exception ledger with its regeneration and verification requirements, plus the additive `runs.context_items` column. The **process** side remains a coordinated API/worker revision boundary — an accepted Run enqueued by an older API and executed by a newer worker still carries parts that worker cannot render — so the runbook in `apps/api/AGENTS.md` and `docs/scaling.md` gains its entry even though the data side is a delete.
- **History loss, accepted**: a chat predating the cutover loses its context parts, so its model-switch boundary stops rendering in the transcript. Accepted because only disposable test data exists; it would not be acceptable once an instance holds real history.
- **Model-visible output changes** for every existing producer: same semantics, new envelope. Existing scenarios in the three modified specs hold apart from the envelope.
- **Out of scope, tracked separately**: non-model effective-context causes and the shipped personalization contradiction (#466); owner-facing rendering of context items (#464) and the Run trajectory viewer (#465); #408's temporal block, Skills, and agentic instruction files land as consumers of this contract; content-bearing auto-recall remains out of scope where #198 placed it.
