- Settings is its own area with a section rail, like Administration:
  Account & security, Appearance, Personalization, Memory and Machines each
  have a page, with Keyboard shortcuts shown as coming soon. On a phone the
  breadcrumb's section name switches sections. Account & security shows the
  signed-in name and email and can sign out every session. `/settings`
  opens Account & security.
- Settings fields share one layout at every width: label, description, then
  the control, with a compact control at the end of the label's line.
  Personalization is a profile card (on/off switch in its header, Save in
  its footer) and a card for what the model provider sees.
- Every form outside the composer saves the same way: Enter in a one-line
  field, Ctrl/⌘+Enter anywhere, and Enter in a multi-line field adds a line.
- Choosing an interface or code font applies it again.
- The settings and administration breadcrumb shows the current section in
  regular weight, as shadcn's breadcrumb does.
