- Keyboard shortcuts have their own settings page. Each shortcut shows its
  keys as key caps; Search, New chat and Attach change with a pencil and
  the next keys pressed, and a changed one offers "Reset to …". Keys
  another changeable shortcut uses offer to swap the two; keys a fixed
  shortcut uses (Enter, Shift+Enter, Esc, the sidebar toggle) are refused
  with what they run. Each shortcut shows how often this browser used it,
  and tooltips and menus show the owner's own bindings.
