- Keyboard shortcuts have their own settings page. Search, New chat and
  Attach can be rebound by pressing new keys, two shortcuts on the same
  keys are both marked, and each shows how often this browser used it.
  Fixed shortcuts (Enter, Shift+Enter, Esc, the sidebar toggle) are listed
  so their keys count for conflicts. Tooltips and menus show the owner's
  own bindings.
