- A long message can be written in a taller composer. Once the message is
  long, an expand button in the input's top-right corner grows it in place,
  animated, to most of the screen, with attachments, the model and Send
  still at hand. Ctrl or
  ⌘+Shift+E toggles it at any length, and sending returns it to its resting
  size. The shortcut can be rebound in Keyboard shortcuts.
