- Administration has Machines and Models sections. Machines shows the
  instance's Machines as the same rows owners see. Models shows each model
  role as its fallback chain, top to bottom (primary, then each fallback,
  with effort and paid marks), and every model grouped by provider and
  searchable: whether it is the default, which roles use it at which step,
  and, opened, its context, prices, inputs, effort levels, dates and links.
  Read-only. Model providers stays a coming-soon section until the instance
  has an administrator role.
