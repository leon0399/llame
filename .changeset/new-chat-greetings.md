- The new-chat greeting varies. The day splits into six hour slots with
  several lines each, weekdays and weekends add their own, and holidays
  and other dates (New Year, Christmas, Halloween, Pi Day, Programmers'
  Day, Friday the 13th and more) have their own lines. One is drawn per
  visit, and without a name a line reads cleanly without it.
