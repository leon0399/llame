- The new-chat greeting varies. The day splits into six hour slots with
  several lines each, weekdays and weekends add their own, and special
  days have their own lines: holidays, nerd dates (Pi Day, Towel Day, Talk
  Like a Pirate Day, Caps Lock Day and more), daylight-saving changes,
  solstices and equinoxes, and Unix-time milestones. One is drawn per
  visit; without a name a line reads cleanly without it.
- Now and then the greeting is a rare one, from 1 in 100 up to 1 in
  100,000, labelled with its odds; the rarest shimmer. Clicking the
  greeting draws another. Under it, a short question invites the first
  message unless the greeting already asks one.
